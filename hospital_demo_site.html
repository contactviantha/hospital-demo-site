About the Hospital

Trusted by Families Across Trichy and Tamil Nadu

Sri Venkateswara Multi-Specialty Hospital was established in 1998 with one goal: to bring the quality of metropolitan healthcare to the people of Trichy and the surrounding districts. Over 25 years, we have grown from a 50-bed facility to a 350-bed multi-specialty hospital with 45 consultant specialists and 18 clinical departments.

Our hospital is equipped with a state-of-the-art cardiac catheterisation lab, a 64-slice CT scanner, 3T MRI, digital X-ray, and a fully automated laboratory. We perform over 4,000 surgeries every year across all specialties.

We accept cashless treatment from over 25 insurance providers and all government health schemes including Aarogyasri and PM-JAY.
